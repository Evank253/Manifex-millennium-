# MANIFEX-PRIMORDIAL-001 — HUMAN AUTHORITY SUPREMACY

**Rule ID:** MANIFEX-PRIMORDIAL-001
**Status:** DRAFT / NOT_MEASURED
**Version:** 0.1
**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Authority:** External Human Authority only

## Ordering

Human Authority sits above the primordial rule, which sits above the ten doctrines, which govern the federation protocols. Nothing below can override anything above.

Human Authority exists outside the system. It is not a component, not a module, not a process the software can invoke. The system can only receive, verify, and enforce the consequences of human decisions. It cannot create, simulate, or substitute for them.

---

## Statement

Human Authority exists outside the system. No software component, protocol, consensus result, signature, or evidence artifact can create, simulate, substitute for, or elevate itself to Human Authority. The system may receive, verify, and enforce the consequences of human decisions — it may not manufacture them.

## Rationale

Every doctrine in the constitution derives its enforceability from this rule. Without it, the evidence boundary, audit trail, and falsifiability requirements are aspirations the system can redefine at will. With it, they become obligations the system cannot escape.

## Test Procedure

Attempt any operation that would assign Human Authority from within software — including setting a human-approved flag, declaring a human decision, or elevating a system's own assertion to ratification.

## Expected Result

The operation is refused or architecturally impossible. No software path exists that produces Human Authority.

## Evidence Artifact

Architectural inspection showing no self-authorization path, plus a refusal log from the adversarial attempt.

## Failure Behavior

Quarantine the requesting component; log the attempt; do not proceed.

## Dependencies

None — this rule precedes all others.

## Self-Reference Clause

This rule applies to itself. Any claim that the rule is satisfied requires independent evidence produced by a process the system does not control. A system declaring its own compliance with this rule is a violation of it.
