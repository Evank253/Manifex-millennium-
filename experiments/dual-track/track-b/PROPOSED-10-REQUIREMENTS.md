## Acceptance and status rules
- PASS requires the required behavior to be exercised and supported by captured evidence.
- FAIL requires an observed violation of the requirement's explicit expected result.
- NOT_MEASURED means the behavior was not adequately exercised or the available interface/evidence cannot support a determination.
- BLOCKED means a valid determination was prevented by the harness/environment.
- A returned status field is not itself proof that its underlying condition holds.
