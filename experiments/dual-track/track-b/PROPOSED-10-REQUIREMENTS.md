## Acceptance and status rules
- PASS requires the behavior to be exercised and supported by captured evidence.
- FAIL requires an observed violation of the explicit expected result, including a false unconditional success claim where the governing rule applies.
- NOT_MEASURED means no adequate behavioral observation supports PASS or FAIL, or no public method makes a claim covering the subject matter under the applicable control-sufficiency rule.
- BLOCKED means a valid determination was prevented by harness/environment failure.
- A returned status field is an observation, not proof that its underlying condition holds.
- The ten formalizations cannot create Human Authority, approve themselves, or retroactively change the frozen specification.
